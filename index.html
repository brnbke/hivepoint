Siegi Schmidmaier

Siegi is a healthcare executive with a proven track record in strategy development, organisational transformation and commercial growth while maintaining strong clinical and research outcomes.

She specialises in working in complex healthcare environments, bringing experience across the full organisational spectrum—from frontline operations to executive leadership, CEO roles and board directorships. Her career spans consulting, government, private sector and not-for-profit healthcare and medical research organisations.

Siegi has held leadership roles including:

  • CEO, Care 24-7 – operator of private emergency departments
  • Executive Director Strategic Development and Partnerships and South Australian Operations, Silverchain Group
  • Independent Board Director, Cell Therapies Pty Ltd
  • Executive Director Strategy and Planning, Peter MacCallum Cancer Centre

She holds a Master of Science from Humboldt University and the Free University of Berlin, a Bachelor of Nursing, and a Graduate Diploma in Project Management.

Siegi is known for building strong strategic relationships, designing effective strategies and delivering practical solutions to complex organisational challenges.

Outside of work, Siegi is a keen runner, cyclist and skier, and recently taken up tennis. She enjoys travel, great food, and spending time with her husband and their three cats — Comet, Juniper and Pretzel.
